# Shield Funding Process Questions

## Application Process Questions for Term Loans, MCA's, LOC's, and Equipment Financing

* **Complete a one page digital application AND Upload 4 BUSINESS BANK STATEMENTS** is all that is required for an approval
* The application process can be completed within 2 hours if all documents are submitted promptly
* After the approval process, to request contracts we require identification and a voided business check or comparable document/screenshot to prepare for the funding wire
* Shield Funding only does soft credit pulls that do not affect your credit
* Shield Funding does not report to credit bureaus
* Some products feature a **10%–100% remaining interest forgiveness** for early prepayment.

## SBA Business Loans Questions/Requirements

### Typical Document Requirements
* Complete online application
* 4 months business bank statements
* 3 years business + personal tax returns
* P&L and balance sheet
* Debt schedule
* A/R and A/P aging

### Important notes: 
* SBA cannot directly refinance MCA debt
* We can structure around it or use conventional
* Must Demonstrate Ability to Repay
* SBA Requires 640+ FICO
* Startups go SBA (typically 700+ credit and fully secured with real estate)

## Invoice Factoring Process Questions/Requirements

### Submit Invoice Factoring Application

* Complete online application
* Provide 4 months of bank statements
* Submit sample invoices and customer list
* Receive decision within 24-48 hours
* No upfront fees or commitments

### Receive Invoice Factoring Approval

* Get your credit line amount and advance rate
* Review and sign factoring agreement
* Complete quick account setup process
* Receive welcome packet with next steps
* Access to online portal activated

### Upload Invoices for Factoring

* Submit invoices through secure online portal
* Include proof of delivery or completion
* Verification completed within hours
* Invoices must be from approved customers
* Track status in real-time dashboard

### Get Invoice Factoring Funds

* Receive funds within 24 hours of approval
* Money deposited directly to your account
* Remaining balance paid when customer pays
* Same Day funding available when needed
