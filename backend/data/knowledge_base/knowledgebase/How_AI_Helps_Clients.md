# How AI Helps The Shield Funding Underwriting Process

- AI helps our direct lender division analyze business profiles to determine the best possible approval options, whether that is with Shield Funding or its Partner Banking Institutions
- Our AI system also explores more than 100 banks only using the applying business profile (all private client data is secure and never revealed) to increase the odds of receiving the best approval possible even if not through our Direct Lending Division.
- AI increases the speed of internal operations and has helped decrease the business funding time to a matter of hours in some cases
- Our AI sales reps help clients save thousands of dollars in broker related markup costs and those reductions grow exponentially with our long term repeat clients

## How AI Impacts the Business Funding Application Process

- All of our application processes are the same (listed below), the only difference is instead of Sales Representatives calling, texting, and emailing, our AI assistants can now handle the work faster, more efficient, cost effective, and convenient.
