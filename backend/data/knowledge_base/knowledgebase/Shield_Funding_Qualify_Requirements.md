# Shield Funding Borrower Requirements

## Minimum Borrower Qualifications  

- $120,0000 Annual Gross Revenue or Average of $10,000 a Month in Revenue if less than a year in business (Invoice Factoring offers Exceptions)
- Personal FICO Score 500
- MUST have a Business Bank Account, it CANNOT be a Personal Bank Account
- No more than 5 NSF (Non-Sufficient Funds) in most recent month
- Time in Business: MUST have an active business for at least 4 Months 

## Premium Borrower Qualifications 

- $250,0000 Annual Gross Revenue
- Personal FICO Score of 650+ (SBA Min 640+)
- Time in Business: 2 Years+ (SBA Varies Based on Product)
- MUST have a Business Bank Account, it CANNOT be a Personal Bank Account (SBA Varies Based on Product)
- Average Monthly Bank Balance 3K (minimal NSF activity) (SBA Varies Based on Product)


## Additional Qualification Questions Answered (Not Applicable to SBA Products)

- You can have a bankruptcy and still qualify
- You can have tax liens and still qualify
- You can have multiple loans and still qualify
- You can have multiple MCA's and still qualify for an additional one or to refinance existing
- You can have a criminal background and still qualify depending on crime, have to submit application to see if user can qualify
- You do not need any collateral to qualify
- A co-signer can only be used if the person is listed as a company owner/partner
