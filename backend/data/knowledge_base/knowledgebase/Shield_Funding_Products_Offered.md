# Shield Funding Products Offered

## Loan Type 1: Merchant Cash Advance 

- Maximum Advance Amount: up to $2,000,000 (no minimum)  
- Rates: Factor Rates range from 1.1 to 1.5  
- Payment Frequency: Daily or Weekly  
- Duration: 3 to 24 months  
- Allowable Uses: No restrictions  
- Early Payment Discounts: Up to 25–100% of interest  

## Loan Type 2: Business Line of Credit
 
- Maximum Credit Limit: up to $200,000 (no minimum) 
- Monthly Interest: 1%–6%  
- Payment Frequency: Monthly  
- Draw Fee: 0–4%  
- Duration: 24 months, renewable  

## Loan Type 3: Term Business Loans

- Maximum Loan Size: up to $2,000,000 (no minimum)  
- Rates: Starting at ~30% APR depending on duration  
- Duration: Up to 48 months  
- Payment Frequency: Weekly or Monthly  

## Loan Type 4: Equipment Financing

- 620 Credit 
- at least 1 year in business
- same day financing, the merchant can walk out with the equipment
- 90% Construction, Some Medical, No Trucks
- Must buy from a new/used dealer
- Typical deals are secured, 5 years, 10-15% annual rate
- Ticket sizes from $10,000 up to multi-million dollar opportunities
- Clients don’t need upfront cash
- Same application process with Shield Funding Term Loans, MCA's and LOC's

## Loan Type 5: Invoice Factoring

- Must be a B2B company selling on terms
- We avoid medical insurance billing, but will look at anything other company type
- A typical client will bill for services rendered or product delivered on terms of 30-90 days
- We evaluate a transaction based on their customer's reputation and history of payments through public records rather than their own credit history or cash situation
- Recent Startups can be approved
- No minimums
- We focus on transactions closer to $100K, but we will consider transactions up to $500K
- No California deals at this time

## Loan Type 6: SBA Loans

- Maximum Loan Amount: up to $15,000,000   
- Rates: ~Prime + 3%  
- Payment Frequency: Monthly  
- Duration: Up to 25 Years 
- Allowable Uses: working capital, acquisitions, partner buyouts, refinance  
- Secured: Can be unsecured under $350K
