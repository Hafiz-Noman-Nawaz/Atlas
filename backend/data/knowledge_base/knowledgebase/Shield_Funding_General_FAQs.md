# Shield Funding — Frequently Asked Questions

## Question: Does applying at Shield Funding affect my credit?
- Answer: We do a soft pull credit inquiry with your application that does not impact your credit.

## Question: I'm buying a business can you help me do that?
- Answer: Unfortunately we can only fund an existing business in your name with a business bank account generating a minimum of $10,000 a month in revenue. If you do not have that we cannot help with funding.

## Question: Can I use my spouses credit if I move forward?
- Answer: Unfortunately if your spouse is not an owner of the business we cannot use their credit profile. If your spouse is an owner it may help in underwriting.

## Question: Do you offer No-Doc Funding Solution?
- Answer: This is the closest to no document funding as we only require a one page application and four recent business bank statements to get you funded.

## Question: What is a merchant cash advance?
- Answer: A merchant cash advance is a financial product that offers business owners an opportunity to get a lump sum of cash from a lender in exchange for a daily or weekly payment until the total principal and fee is paid back. 

## Question: What is the total cost of Shield Funding Business Funding?
- Answer: There are several funding products, but our term loans start at 30% APR and our line of credit options start at about 14% annual finance charge.

## Question: Which types of lenders offer Alternative Business Loans or Merchant Cash Advances?
- Answer: Most online lenders excluding banks and credit unions.

## Question: Can I get a merchant cash advance from a bank?
- Answer: No.

## Question: Do I pay interest on a merchant cash advance?
- Answer: No. There is no compounding interest, it is just one set fee to purchase sales from the business. However, the cost is clearly separated and can be looked at in the lens of an interest expense or a fee for getting cash in a crunch.

## Question: How do I calculate what I’ll pay for a merchant cash advance?
- Answer: It depends on the factor rate you receive. An example is a 1.3 factor rate on an advance of ten thousand dollars will be a payback of thirteen thousand dollars, the principal ten thousand and the fee of three thousand.

## Question: How does Shield Funding take their repayment?
- Answer: There are a few ways that can be structured depending on how your company receives revenue, but these questions will all be answered by your sales representative before you take any funding. Generally, you will get an ACH auto withdrawal payment that is daily, weekly, or monthly depending on the financial product.

## Question: Does Shield Funding report to credit bureaus?
- Answer: For most products, No. Most alternative lenders do not report to credit bureaus, however, this is starting to change so some financial products offered by Shield Funding may get reported by our third party partners.

## Question: How do I apply for a merchant cash advance?
- Answer: You can apply online at 'shieldfunding.com' or by calling directly '(888) 882-6117' 24 hours a day seven days a week.

## Question: Can I get a merchant cash advance with poor credit?
- Answer: Yes, merchant cash advances are ideal for small business owners with poor credit. Lenders will work with borrowers who have credit scores as low as 500 or possibly less.

## Question: Can I refinance a merchant cash advance?
- Answer: Yes, if your sales volume supports it, you may be eligible to refinance your existing MCA or consolidate multiple MCAs into one. Generally you need 25%-50% paid in to request renewals.

## Question: How is a merchant cash advance different from a business loan?
- Answer: Unlike a loan with fixed monthly payments, an MCA is repaid through a percentage of daily or weekly sales, making it more flexible during slow sales periods. There are term loans available that share most of the traditional loan structure if you qualify.

## Question: What are the risks of taking a merchant cash advance?
- Answer: Risks include higher fees than traditional financing from a bank, and potential cash flow challenges due to daily or weekly repayments.

## Question: Are merchant cash advances regulated?
- Answer: MCAs are not subject to the same regulations as traditional loans, which makes terms and practices vary significantly between providers. Make sure you work with a reputable lender with a track record and reviews like Shield Funding.

## Question: Can I apply for funding if my business has a discharged bankruptcy on record?
- Answer: Yes.

## Question: How will funding affect my cash flow?
- Answer: Because your business will have a new payment it will decrease cash flow, but before taking the funding both Shield Funding and the borrower will determine a comfortable payment plan to reduce potential cash flow issues.

## Question: What happens if I can’t repay the advance?
- Answer: Every form of borrowing has some consequences for failing to pay, and it is usually the standard debt collection procedures for most types of borrowing. Ask your sales representative before taking the funding if you have any concerns about this.

## Question: Are there restrictions on how I can use the funds?
- Answer: There are no restrictions, use the funds any way you want.

## Question: What happens if I switch credit card processors?
- Answer: Most lenders today do not require you to switch credit card processors. In the event that you do in an effort to get better payment structure there will be a contract that lays out your options in the event you want to switch processors.

## Question: Can the terms of the agreement be renegotiated?
- Answer: There is flexibility to revise repayment terms if your business circumstances change significantly.

## Question: What is Shield Funding’s track record and reputation?

The company has an **A+ rating with the Better Business Bureau**, it has **4.9 out of 5 stars for almost 1,000 verified reviews on Trustpilot**, and the company is successful because of white-glove service and the speed and ease of funding.

## Question: Are there penalties for missing a payment or having insufficient funds in my account?
- Answer: Each lender and each contract can be set up differently, so before taking the funding please look at your contract to see an outline if any penalty exists for failed payments.

## Question: What recourse do I have if I find discrepancies or errors in the ACH debits?
- Answer: Any mistakes by the lender will be refunded to the client immediately.

## Question: Are there any restrictions on selling or transferring my business while this agreement is active?
- Answer: Yes. Just like any other financial product the borrower is responsible for payback.

## Question: Is there a cap on the maximum funding amount available?
- Answer: 2 million dollars is generally the cap on advances although there are always exceptions.

## Question: Are there restrictions on the maximum number of negative banking days allowed per month?
- Answer: Yes, but there are exceptions. Once you have more than 2 negative days in your most recent business banking month the more limited your funding options can become, although there are always exceptions depending on the overall borrowing business profile.

## Question: Which industries are classified as "preferred," and how does this affect the approval process?
- Answer: There is no “preferred” industry although certain high cash flow business models are ideal for merchant cash advances or certain types of financing products.

## Question: Are there any restricted industries that are not eligible for an advance?
- Answer: There are almost no industry restrictions so you are encouraged to apply, there is nothing to lose as there are no upfront costs.

## Question: What is the origination fee, and how is it applied to the funded amount?
- Answer: The origination fee is an administrative cost as a result of the work that goes into underwriting the file. The origination fee, if required, is paid out of the advance amount at the time of funding.

## Question: Are there background checks involved, and what factors could lead to disqualification?
- Answer: There can be background checks for some larger loans, and financial crimes or violent offenses can potentially disqualify a borrower.

## Question: Does Shield Funding fund businesses with recent tax liens?
- Answer: Yes. Your business can receive funding with existing tax liens.

## Question: Does the lender consider seasonal fluctuations in revenue during underwriting?
- Answer: Yes. Shield Funding understands all industry types after 2 decades of funding businesses so they already understand the impact of seasons on particular types of businesses.

## Question: Does having a co-signer with stronger credit improve my chances of approval?
- Answer: There can only be a co-signer if that person is an owner in the business. And yes that person can have a positive impact on your approval options if they have a good credit profile.

## Question: What role does my business’s online presence play in the approval process?
- Answer: The only impact it has is if you have a very bad profile from customers, which may not inspire confidence when underwriting your file.

## Question: Are there any limitations on applying if my business operates out of multiple locations?
- Answer: No.

## Question: How does the lender handle businesses with prior funding that is not yet fully repaid?
- Answer: Each situation is different and it depends on the lender of the existing position or positions. However, it can be either the original lender or lenders are paid out of the new funding or the client can receive the entire new funding on top of the existing funding positions with no effect.

## Question: What safeguards does the lender have in place to prevent over-debiting from my business account?
- Answer: The payment amount is set in the beginning and is locked in place so there will be no over-debiting.

## Question: Can my funding amount be increased later if my business revenue grows?
- Answer: Yes. Any needs for additional funding should be discussed with your Shield Funding representative.

## Question: What are the lender's policies regarding businesses with international operations or accounts?
- Answer: We generally do not fund businesses outside of the United States. As long as you have a local business operation with a business bank account we can fund that without any concern for your additional operations.

## Question: What is the minimum amount of deposits required to be eligible for funding?
- Answer: There is no set minimum however the more deposits the better.

## Question: What does it mean that the business must net 50% of proceeds, and how is this calculated?
- Answer: This refers to the possibility of a client that has additional positions and requires that lender to be paid out of the new funding. That amount should not be more than 50% of the new funding.

## Question: Does monthly reporting to D&B and Experian affect my business credit positively, and what should I expect?
- Answer: When applying for a business loan the credit requirements are only one small part of the application process with Shield Funding, however, a better credit profile always improves your underwriting outcome when seeking larger amounts at the best rates.

## Question: Do I have to process credit cards to receive an advance?
- Answer: No. You can receive a merchant cash advance based on sales revenue from other sources such as checks from customers or other types of payments to your business bank account.

## Question: What is split withholding?
- Answer: It’s a repayment method where the credit card processor splits your credit card receipts between you and the MCA provider, sending your payment automatically.

## Question: Is a business plan or financial projection required?
- Answer: No.

## Question: How does the provider evaluate my eligibility?
- Answer: Shield Funding business loans consider your monthly gross revenue, consistent deposits, and overall cash flow. Additionally we look at soft credit profile, NSF’s, daily balance averages, and time in business. The better the profile, the better rates and terms with larger approval amounts.

## Question: What are the criteria for buyouts or second positions, and how does this impact eligibility?
- Answer: Having current positions does not disqualify you, but the amount and history of those will be looked at. Many clients at Shield Funding have multiple positions. 

## Question: How does an MCA impact my credit score?
- Answer: An MCA typically does not impact your credit score because it is business funding and not on your personal credit, and a soft pull is done to get started.

## Question: Can startups qualify for an MCA?
- Answer: Startups or new businesses do not qualify for funding. You must have a business for 4 months with a business bank account with an average of $10,000 in monthly revenue. Personal accounts do not qualify. 

## Question: Do I need to have a business checking account to qualify?
- Answer: Yes. You must have a business bank account to qualify, you cannot use a personal bank account to apply.

## Question: Are there any additional fees?
- Answer: There can be origination fees and other administrative costs depending on the size of funding or the financial product chosen.

## Question: What is the typical repayment period?
- Answer: 6 months to 3 years is the general range for merchant cash advances, however there have been 4-year options as well as renewable lines of credit. Equipment loans can go 5 years or longer.

## Question: Are there additional fees not disclosed in the itemization of amount funded (hidden fees)?
- Answer: There are no hidden fees with Shield Funding, all fees will be disclosed before the funding is taken.

## Question: How is the repayment structured?
- Answer: Shield Funding offers several products that feature daily, weekly, or monthly payments. Daily payments are only Monday through Friday, there are no weekend payments. The amount of the payment can be based on the financial product, the amount of the funding, the length of time established for the funding, and the agreed upon payment amount that works with the borrower’s cash flow needs.

## Question: What happens if my sales fluctuate or decrease?
- Answer: Payment structures may be adjusted if the borrowing company sales revenue decreases significantly. Shield Funding can offer a buyer protection clause to lower payments if you hit a cycle with lower revenue, and if you miss a payment we are not going to break your business like banks that may freeze your account.

## Question: Is there a prepayment penalty?
- Answer: There is no prepayment penalty, some products feature a **10%–100% remaining interest forgiveness**.

## Question: What percentage of my daily sales will be deducted?
- Answer: With a merchant cash advance the payback rate or holdback rate is determined as a percentage of revenue. That percentage is determined by affordability based on cash flows of the business, but it can be anywhere from 5–30% or more depending on the size and duration of the advance and the overall factor rate, but the average is approximately 10%.

## Question: Are there any required minimum sales volumes during repayment?

## Question: What is the process for requesting a payment adjustment if the daily payments need an adjustment?

## Question: How is the daily or weekly repayment amount calculated, and can it fluctuate?

Each funding product is different but the goal is to have the client receive a payment amount that is comfortable and easy to pay regularly without affecting the business cash flow.

## Question: Are there any incentives, such as lower factor rates, for returning customers or strong repayment history?
- Answer: Absolutely. You will almost always receive improvements in rates and terms with consistent payment history.

## Question: What is the ACH withdrawal repayment method?
